Index Work System Design Ergonomics

Developed by Saranraj Ramachandran, DoIE, CEG, AU

Built for Industrial Engineering coursework - syllabus-mapped to Anna University IE23301 (Work System Design) & IE23401 (Applied Ergonomics), and cross-checked against ILO's Introduction to Work Study and standard human-factors references. Scoring simulators (RULA, REBA, OWAS, frequency multiplier in NIOSH) use simplified teaching models built on the official angle bands and action-level thresholds - cross-check borderline real assessments against the published worksheets cited in each experiment's References.
