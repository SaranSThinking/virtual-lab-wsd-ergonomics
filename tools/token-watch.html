    Most expensive chats and how to cut them

    Habits that cut usage

    • One task per chat. Start fresh, or /clear, when the topic changes.
    • Every reply re-reads the whole chat. Long chats cost far more than many short ones; use /compact or paste a short summary into a new chat.
    • Paste only the part of a file or log you need. Big pastes and attachments are re-read on every later reply.
    • Project knowledge files load into every chat in that project. Keep them short and relevant.
    • Use a smaller model (Sonnet or Haiku) for routine edits and questions.
    • Turn off connectors and plugins you are not using. Their tool lists are sent with every message.
    • Answer waiting chats or close them. Coming back after a long pause rebuilds the cache at full price.

    Cost = weighted tokens: fresh input 1, cache write 1.25 to 2, cache read 0.1, output 5. For claude.ai exports the numbers are estimates (characters ÷ 4, each reply re-reading the chat and project knowledge). Use them to rank, not to bill.
